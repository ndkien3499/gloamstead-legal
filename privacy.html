Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective 25 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Gloamstead game ("the Game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The Game is run by Nguyễn Đồng Kiên, an individual developer resident in Vietnam publishing as Lowpine ("we", "us"), who is responsible for your data. Contact: kiennd99.work@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash; we cannot read it.
  • Profile and game data: your player name, character appearance, items, progress, purchases of in-game items with gold, trades at the Market, gift code redemptions, and a log of what happens in play (needed to keep the game fair and fix problems).
  • Purchase data: if you buy something for real money, the store sends us a receipt (product, time, transaction ID). We never receive your card or bank details. We also record each opening of a random reward box.
  • Technical data: IP address, device model, operating system, app version and server logs, collected automatically when your device talks to our servers.
  • Crash reports: when the Game crashes or hits a serious error, Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service) sends us a report with the error details, device model, operating system, app version, a random installation ID and the time. Crash reports do not contain your email or password.

We do not use advertising or ad tracking in the Game, and we do not sell your data.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in (performing our contract with you).
  • To run the Game, save your progress and deliver items (contract).
  • To prevent cheating and fraud, and keep the Game stable (our legitimate interest in a fair, working game).
  • To keep purchase and box-opening records where the law requires it (legal obligation).
  • To answer you when you contact us.

3. What other players see

Your player name, appearance and equipped gear can be seen by other players, for example on leaderboards. Your email is never shown to other players.

4. Who we share it with

  • Hosting providers that run our servers and store the data for us.
  • Apple and Google, to process purchases you make through their stores.
  • Google (Firebase Crashlytics), to collect and show us crash reports.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Our servers may be located outside your country. When data leaves the EU/UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the providers' standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and game data: until you delete your account.
  • Server logs and crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Purchase and box-opening records: as long as tax and consumer laws require, even after the account is deleted.
  • Backups: up to 30 days after deletion, then overwritten.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the Game: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account, characters and leaderboard entries straight away.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it by email. We reply within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may also have the right to object to or restrict some uses, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, passwords are hashed, and sign-in tokens on your phone are kept in the system's secure storage (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain). No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you and the authorities about a serious breach as the law requires.

9. Changes

We will post any update here with a new version number and date, and tell you in the Game if the change is significant.
